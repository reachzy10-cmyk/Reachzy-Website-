SECTION 01

Eyebrow:
The part that matters

Finding creators is easy. Knowing which ones deserve a conversation is harder.

A creator can have 500,000 subscribers and still be completely wrong for a software product.

The useful questions come before the outreach:

What does this creator actually make?

Who watches it?

Why do those people care?

Would your product make sense in that content?

Has the creator already worked with products in this world?

That is the work Reachzy puts behind creator discovery.


---

SECTION 02

Eyebrow:
How we look at a campaign

We start with the product, then work backwards to the creator.

A campaign begins with understanding what the brand is actually selling and who it needs to reach.

01 — The product

What does it do?
Who is it for?
Why would someone choose it?

02 — The audience

Who already watches the kind of content your product belongs in?

03 — The creator

What does the creator make repeatedly?
How do they explain products?
Would your product feel natural on the channel?

04 — The commercial history

Has the creator worked with brands before?
What kinds of products have appeared on the channel?
What evidence is available?

05 — The opportunity

Only then does the conversation become:

What could this collaboration actually look like?

This is the thinking behind a creator recommendation — not a subscriber search with a brand name attached.
