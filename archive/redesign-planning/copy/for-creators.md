Final copy

This version takes the strongest material from the creator partnership deck—relevant opportunities, less chasing, clear deal information, creator approval, and a defined four-step process—and turns it into a more convincing website experience.  

The main change is that the page does not keep telling creators that Reachzy is “relevant,” “clear,” or “creator-first.” It shows what that actually means. That is consistent with the Reachzy standard: specific explanations over generic agency language, real capabilities over assumed ones, and credibility over perceived scale.

SECTION 01

Eyebrow

Why Reachzy exists

You already spend enough time running the channel.

There is the content itself.

Then there are the emails, follow-ups, brand requests, vague proposals, questions about deliverables, and conversations that go nowhere.

Reachzy exists to take some of that work away from the creator side.

We look for sponsorship opportunities, filter them around your content and audience, and bring the ones worth considering to you.

The goal is not to put more offers in your inbox.

It is to make the offers that do reach you worth opening. 


---

SECTION 02

Eyebrow

Before an opportunity reaches you

We have to understand why the brand belongs on your channel.

A brand looking for “tech creators” is not enough.

We look at the product, what the brand is trying to promote, the kind of content you make, the audience around that content, and what the collaboration would actually ask you to create.

That means a sponsorship can be rejected before it ever reaches you.

The aim is simple:

There should be a reason the brand contacted your channel specifically.

This reflects the deck's emphasis on opportunities that connect with a creator's niche, audience and content style rather than random outreach.

SECTION 03

Eyebrow

What you receive

You should know what the opportunity is before deciding whether you want it.

When Reachzy brings you an opportunity, the important information should be clear from the beginning.

The brand

Who is behind the opportunity and what are they promoting?

The product

What exactly would you be talking about?

The collaboration

What is the brand looking for from your channel?

The commercial terms

What does the opportunity involve from your side?

The timeline

When would the collaboration need to happen?

The next step

What happens if you are interested?

The creator deck specifically establishes this expectation: the creator sees the brand, idea and expectations before choosing whether to move forward.

SECTION 04

Eyebrow

The process

You see the opportunity first. Then you decide.

01 — We find the opportunity

We identify brands and products that have a reasonable connection to your content and audience.

02 — We bring it to you

You receive the relevant details before anything is treated as agreed.

03 — You decide

Interested? Continue the conversation.

Not interested? Decline it.

Need more information? Ask.

The choice stays with you. 

04 — We stay involved

When you choose to continue, Reachzy helps keep communication between the creator and brand clear as the opportunity develops.

The exact scope depends on the campaign and what Reachzy is handling.

The major improvement here

The page's central promise is now observable:
