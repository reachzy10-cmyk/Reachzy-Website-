The idea behind Reachzy

A large audience is easy to find. The right reason to care is harder.

Subscriber count tells you how many people follow a creator.

It does not tell you why they follow them, what they use, what they buy, or whether your product belongs in the content they come for.

That is where Reachzy starts.

We look at the product, the creator, the audience and the reason the collaboration should exist in the first place.


---

What we do

We bring the product and the creator into the same conversation.

A brand gives us the product, the audience it wants to reach and what it is trying to achieve.

We research creators who make sense for that brief, approach the ones worth talking to, and develop the opportunity with both sides in mind.

For creators, the process works from the other direction. We look for sponsorship opportunities that belong in their content, bring them the details, and let them decide whether they want to take the conversation forward.

When there is a real opportunity on both sides, Reachzy helps move the collaboration forward.


---

The process

It starts with understanding the brief.

01 — Understand the product

What does it do? Who is it for? Why would someone use it?

02 — Find the creators

We look for creators whose content and audience have a genuine connection to the product.

03 — Start the conversation

We approach the relevant creators and find out whether the opportunity makes sense to them too.

04 — Build the collaboration

The campaign takes shape around the brief, the creator's content and what both sides agree to.
