# IMPRIO creator page brief — 26 November 2026

This is the content guide for the first creator pages. The [intake record](creator-intake-2026-11-26.md) holds the original messages, full rate wording and internal contact details. Confirm each page, portrait, published price and IMPRIO enquiry route with the creator before launch.

## Page structure for every creator

1. **Who they are:** name, portrait, concise bio, content topics and language.
2. **Where brands can see the work:** official channel links, dated audience counts and two or three representative posts.
3. **Why they fit:** product categories that naturally belong in their content, explained with linked examples.
4. **Ways to work together:** available formats, approved prices and confirmed inclusions.
5. **Relevant experience:** verified collaboration or product-demo links, described only as strongly as the evidence permits.
6. **Next step:** one clear IMPRIO enquiry button and what happens after a brand sends a brief.

Do not put a general “average views” claim on the first page. The public view counts available today do not have a consistent reporting period across uploads. If a creator wants a performance figure shown, ask for a current, format-specific metric with a stated period. A channel count and strong examples are enough to start.
