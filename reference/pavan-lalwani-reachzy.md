SECTION 06 — HOW COLLABORATIONS WORK
==================================================

Eyebrow:
A clear process from brief to content

Headline:
Plan the campaign before production begins.

Campaign brief
Share the product, target audience, key messages, intended use
cases, deliverables and desired launch window.

Content planning
The team reviews the brief and confirms feasibility and expected
timing based on research needs, production complexity and the
existing content schedule.

Script development
The creator's team develops the script to keep the content
educational, practical and aligned with the audience.

Review before recording
The team can share the script for brand review before recording.

Final scope and terms
Deliverables, commercial terms, usage rights, whitelisting and any
category or competitor restrictions are discussed and agreed
before proceeding.

Closing line:
A useful brief gives everyone a clearer starting point and helps
set realistic expectations for the finished content.
